Data Privacy Notice

Who collects your data: AgapAlert, operated by the Barangay Dalongue Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (DRRMC), Santa Barbara, Pangasinan.

What we collect: Full name, mobile number, email address, and location data (only during an active SOS or report).

Why we collect it: To send flood alerts, dispatch rescue, manage evacuation centers, and contact you in an emergency.

Who can access it: Barangay officials and administrators operating AgapAlert. Data is not sold or shared with commercial third parties.

How long we keep it: For as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting the barangay DRRMC.

Your rights under R.A. 10173: You have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data. Contact: mdrrmo@santabarbara.gov.ph
